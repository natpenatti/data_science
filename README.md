# Natasha Penatti
<sub>*Biologist | PhD in Geosciences | Remote Sensing Specialist | Data Science & Computer Vision Student*</sub>

As a Biologist holding a Master's in Ecology and Conservation (UFMS) and a PhD in Geosciences from the University of São Paulo (USP), my academic research focused on applying remote sensing to solve complex environmental challenges, analyzing large-scale hydrological and phenological dynamics across diverse ecosystems.

I bring over 6.5 years of practical experience as a Remote Sensing Specialist in the agribusiness sector. My work has involved precision agriculture, continuous monitoring through satellite time-series analysis, and the evaluation of soil data. I have also played a key role in developing geospatial products for rural credit and insurance, encompassing database structuring, risk analysis, and spatial intelligence for decision-making.

Currently, I am expanding my technical scope by pursuing a Postgraduate degree in Computer Vision and Deep Learning, alongside practical training in Python and Data Science. My primary goal is to integrate geospatial data with advanced, scalable computational models and AI-driven solutions.

**My main interests include:**
* Geospatial Data Science
* Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
* Computer Vision applied to environmental analysis
* AgTech & Environmental Intelligence
* Machine Learning for spatial and temporal data

**Background in:** Remote Sensing, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Geosciences, Environmental Monitoring, AgTech, and Python.
